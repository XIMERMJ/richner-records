01 / THE PUBLICATION

Richner Records is a new quarterly independent publication under Richner Records LLC, created for thoughtful artist conversations and close attention to the culture around the work.

It begins as a digital-first platform, with future physical editions envisioned as objects to keep, revisit and share.

02 / THE PERSPECTIVE

Founded by John Richner, it approaches interviews artist to artist: attentive to process, uncertainty, craft and the reality behind finished work.

No publicity copy. No rapid-cycle coverage. Clear, curious editorial work with room for context and personality.
