Richner Records — Culture / Consumption

We've Grown So Used to Consuming That We've Forgotten How to Experience.

What happens when everything we love becomes something we're expected to finish, replace, and move on from?

We've grown so used to consuming that we've forgotten how to experience. Music arrives at midnight, and before we've even had the chance to live with it, we're already asking an artist what comes next. Albums become release weeks. Songs become fifteen-second moments. Art that may have taken months or years to create can enter our lives and disappear from our attention in a matter of days. This isn't an accusation against the listener — I'm a listener too. It's a question about all of us. At what point did experiencing something become less important than simply consuming the next thing?
